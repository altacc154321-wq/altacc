THE FIELD MANUAL

Know your piece.
Own the board.

Two human players. One piece each. Real-time combat on an 8×8 board. Reduce your rival’s health to zero to claim victory.

Q Target a legal moveE Target a legal strikeCLICK Commit on the board or mapSPACE Strike your rival’s squareDRAG Rotate the camera↑ ↓ ← → Select tile · Enter to commit

Teal tiles show legal movement. Red tiles show legal attacks. Attacks have a visible windup: move before impact to evade. Sliding pieces cannot pass through a rival. Knights leap over them. Every attack returns to its origin. Pawns cannot retreat or promote; plan your advance carefully. Mutually unreachable pieces draw automatically so neither player is stranded.

Controller: left stick selects squares, right stick rotates the camera, A commits, X targets attacks, Y targets movement. No unrestricted running.
